Scene 02 — A note from the director

Some places keep waiting long after we leave.

I grew up two stops from a station like Kisaragi. When the line closed, nobody took down the timetable. The paint faded, the cicadas got louder, and the platform kept its evening light as if a train might still come.

The film is about Hana, seventeen, who spends one last summer there before moving to the city. It is a quiet story: clouds, grass, a radio, a girl who is not ready to say goodbye.

— Rin Okada, director

Scene 03 — Kisaragi Station, 6:40 pm

The rails still hum at dusk.

She counts cicadas instead of minutes.
